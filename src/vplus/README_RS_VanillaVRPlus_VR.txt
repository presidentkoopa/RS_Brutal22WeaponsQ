RS_VanillaVRPlus_VR v9 -- VR hands, real VR reloading and ballistics for Vanilla VR Plus
(1.0b_Weapons_VanillaVRPlus_v1.2) on stock QuestZDoom 17.x. No engine changes.

Load after the weapon set. Options: Options > RS VR Hands, Reload, Ballistics
(also at the bottom of the set's 3D Weapon Settings).

  VR hands    Ermac's posed hands on every gun; bring your off hand to the front
              of the gun and squeeze the grip to hold it (the engine's own
              two-hand steadying, left as it is).
  Reloading   VR (default) on the VR_SuperShotgun, VR_Revolver, VR_Revolver2, M16, VR_Shotgun; every other gun, and Stock, is the
              set's own reload.
              VR reloading: reload drops the magazine. Take a
              new one with the off-hand trigger, or with the off
              grip at least 50 cm from the gun (closer, the grip
              steadies the gun as usual). Put it under the well
              and push it home. When the gun needs it, take the
              handle or slide with the off trigger, pull it all
              the way back and let go.   all the way back and
              let go. Pump shotguns, VR: reload, then take a
              shell away from the gun (off trigger, or grip away
              from the gun), put it under the loading port and
              push it in. With your hand on the pump (steadied,
              or the off trigger held on it), pull it back and
              push it forward after each shot.   Break-open guns
              and revolvers, VR: reload opens the gun. Take a
              shell or round (off trigger, or grip away from the
              gun), put it in a chamber from behind and push it
              home. Flick the gun shut, or push it shut with the
              off hand.
  Ballistics  Enhanced: impacts throw dust, sparks and chips by surface.

Files: Decorate/revolver.txt, rifle.txt and shotty.txt are the set's own files with the
default weapon-switch fix (with Weapon switching "Default", switching away from the
M16, a revolver or the pump shotgun crashed QuestZDoom; they now lower and raise the
way the set's fist does); Decorate/revolver.txt, Decorate/rifle.txt,
Decorate/shotty.txt also carries the real VR reload's states.
They replace the set's copies while this pack is loaded.
Do not load together with BD22_Models (they share these scripts).

Hands and poses: Ermac, Rusted Legacy / Force Unleashed (MIT,
models/rsq/LICENSE_RustedLegacy_MIT.txt).
